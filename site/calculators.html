The calculators_

Three questions a project has to answer before it starts — is the brief good enough, what does one person's hour cost, what does a whole team cost — each with an open formula instead of a private opinion.

01

Brief Bar

Is this brief good enough to send to a team?

A score out of 100 from fourteen public rules, and a separate gate that can refuse publication whatever the score. A model judges each rule; code owns every number.

The client rate split into what the person takes home and what holds the structure up — set by how much of the work they cover and how independently they deliver it.

Team Rate

What does a team cost, priced as one?

Roles, their share of the effort, the level each is delivered at and where each member lives — in; one blended rate the client sees, and a check that no one is squeezed below their own — out.
