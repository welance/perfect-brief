bring your own key

With your key, we run no model for you.

Put an OpenRouter key in the x-llm-key header. The call runs on your account, skips our cache, and may name any of six allowed models: DeepSeek V4 Pro and Flash, Gemini Flash-Lite, GPT-5 nano, Claude Haiku 4.5. Use a spend-capped key.

bash · your own judge
curl -X POST https://briefs.welance.com/v1/score \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -H 'x-llm-key: sk-or-…' \
  -d '{"brief":"…","judge":"llm","model":"deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro"}'

The MCP server reads the same key from PB_LLM_KEY, so your assistant scores on your account.

Every line of ours that touches your key is on its own page, with the test that fails if it ever changes.
