Perfect Team_

A cross-functional team, priced the way a single independent is priced. The roles and their weights describe the project; each role carries its own level and cost base. The client sees one blended rate — not a hundred different ones — margins vary inside the team by level, and the project as a whole clears a stated, named welance margin anyone can check.

The team, role by role

The name is the team's, not a person's. For each role: its weight in the project, the level it is delivered at, the member's cost-of-living coefficient, and their own rate — for the no-deal check.

Si parte! — the first check

The formulas above produce the starting team. What they cannot see — compatibility between members and with the client, kindness, availability, how someone carries stress — is learned only by working together.

A suggested rhythm, deliberately not a formula. Work starts for real, with the declared reserve to replace, remove or modify the initial choices — and it is bidirectional, always: the independent may not feel right either. Every human problem should surface at the first check, not fester. The adjustment is made on the most important asset: the people.

The four bands must sum to the client rate — that is the equation. Margin the people leave on the table (headroom) and margin that comes from where somebody lives (the geographic differential) are shown in their own colours, because margin that cannot be seen cannot be argued with.

Where the differential goes matters. If it silently becomes margin, the collective profits from where someone lives. Name its destination — reserve, solidarity pool, reinvestment — so it can be argued about rather than assumed.

No-deal rule. If a member's rate exceeds what their level allows at the agreed client rate, the engagement does not happen. Nobody is squeezed below their rate to make the margin work.

Coefficients are editable defaults and need a real source (OECD PPP, World Bank ICP, Numbeo) before anyone is paid from them.
