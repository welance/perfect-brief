## Things worth not rediscovering

- **The live LLM judge is dead in production.** `POST /v1/score` with
  `judge=llm` returns 504 after ~15.15s on both prod and staging — an ingress
  read timeout, not Cloudflare (whose own is ~100s). Fixing the timeout is
  platform config (tenants repo) and is still open.
  The console no longer hides it. `MODE` (the judge the reader asked for) and
  `SRC` (the judge that produced what is on screen) are now separate: a failed
  live call sets `SRC="mock"`, re-runs the keyword judge, warns `liveFail`, and
  leaves a standing `#vsrc` line under the score — `srcMock`, "keyword score —
  the AI judge did not answer". **It must not drop the reader out of live
  mode**: the first attempt did, and that hides `#byok` and the model selector,
  which is precisely what someone whose live call just failed reaches for. Two
  tests hold that down — one on the provenance line, one on the key field and
  the retry button surviving a 504. (`aiFail` still exists and is still honest —
  it belongs to the *suggestion* path, where the built-in options really are
  shown.)
- **Free AI is already ungated.** The server holds an OpenRouter key
  (`llm_configured: true`) and `judge=llm` needs no BYOK. Only
  `PB_DEFAULT_JUDGE=mock` and the broken ingress stand between a visitor and
  welance's credit — 60 req/min per IP, and `/v1/suggest/all` costs up to ~6 LLM
  calls per request. **Put a credit cap on the OpenRouter key, and land a quota,
  before the ingress is fixed.** Doing the timeout first goes from "broken" to
  "open bar".
- **`code.js` owns code blocks.** It only processes `.code` containers, token
  colours are scoped `.code .s` / `.code .kw`, and it rewrites the text of
  anything wearing `.code-copy` (so an "open →" link must use another class).
  Its guarantee — highlighting applied at runtime, copy yields clean source — is
  why the start chips use it instead of a second highlighter.
- **SVG text does not wrap.** The seam figure translates through the normal
  `data-i18n` path (`applyNodes` uses `textContent`, which works on SVG `<text>`),
  but every label has to stay short in all nine languages. On phones the figure
  keeps `min-width: 760px` and scrolls inside its band rather than shrinking to
  ~7px.
- **`.hl` is not a link style.** It marks the "change the rules" invitation, and
  a test asserts exactly one exists pointing at GOVERNANCE.md.
- **The gate is four rules**: `clear-title`, `problem-defined`, `budget-floor`
  (not_fail) and `anonymised` (pass). `timeline` is **not** one of them — a brief
  with no deadline loses points and still publishes. Any illustration showing a
  blocked brief must fail a gate rule; the canonical example is the `blocked`
  preset: 92/100, `Directory-ready`, blocked on `anonymised`.

  **The verdict panel reads badly on exactly that preset.**
  It stacks a large score above a small red gate line, so it reads as a single
  contradictory statement rather than two independent axes: how good the brief
  is, and whether it may publish. The engine is right (92.0 / Directory-ready /
  blocked / missing `["anonymised"]`, verified against production); the panel is
  what needs separating. Left alone deliberately — it is a design decision, not
  a bug, and worth taking properly.

## Open, in the order I would do them

1. **Credit cap on the OpenRouter key.** Two minutes, no deploy. Nothing below
   is safe until this exists.
2. **Free-tries quota** in Redis, before the ingress is fixed. Still undecided,
   and the decision is the user's: how many tries, and counted against IP /
   cookie / `localStorage` token. With a spend cap in place this is politeness,
   not enforcement.
3. **The 15s ingress timeout** (tenants repo, stops at an MR). Doing this before
   1 and 2 goes from "broken" to "open bar".
4. **Separate the score axis from the gate axis** in the console verdict panel
   (above).
5. **1.7.0** once branding has landed — the two lines of work are interleaved in
   `site/`, so the release carries both.
